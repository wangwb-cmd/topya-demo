Privacy Policy

Halo VPN · Updated 2 November 2026

1. About this policy

Halo VPN is an Android app for selecting a VPN location and starting a connection. This policy explains the device permissions and connection information involved when you use it.

2. Permission to connect

Android presents a VPN authorization request before Halo can establish a tunnel. You may refuse that request, in which case no tunnel starts. Android may also present a notification permission prompt so the app can display its status. This notification choice is independent of VPN authorization.

3. Choices kept on your device

Your selected location can be saved on the device and restored if it remains valid. Halo's recent connection history also remains on the device, with a design limit of 20 entries. Removing app data or uninstalling can remove that history. These records are not described as a cloud backup.

4. Service session and routing

Halo can use a guest session without asking you to enter an email address. The app contacts its service backend for connection settings and available nodes. That exchange can involve a service identifier, so an email-free screen should not be understood as the absence of an account-related identifier.

5. Operational records

Depending on the configuration in use, the app may send information that helps operate the VPN and diagnose connection results. Such information can cover guest or user level, device fingerprint and characteristics, region, VPN exit IP, node and routing context, connection and disconnection outcomes, and advertising-related events. A report may be queued on the device before submission. Halo does not promise that every layer of the VPN network keeps no operational records.

6. Advertising components

An ad orchestration path named TopOn and an event reporting component named AdProfitHub are part of the app flow. Advertising services enabled for a connection may process device or interaction details used for ad delivery, event measurement, and troubleshooting. An ad request does not mean an ad will appear each time.

7. Control and updates

You may clear local app data through Android and may decline VPN or notification permission. Halo is not directed at children. If the way the service handles information changes materially, this policy will be updated.

These terms apply when you use the Halo VPN Android app. Using the service means that you accept them. Please stop using Halo if you do not agree.

2. Connection sessions

Halo offers a choice of available VPN locations. Its session design allows up to 90 minutes per new connection without a session traffic quota. Changing locations during an active session is designed to keep the remaining time. The locations and session state shown in the app determine what is available at that moment.

3. Starting a tunnel

Approval of Android's VPN request is necessary before a tunnel can be created. Notification permission is separate. Selecting a location or seeing a route check does not, on its own, establish a working VPN tunnel.

4. Ads within the flow

The app can request advertising during a connection attempt. Ads are not assured on every attempt, and viewing one is not a promise that a route will connect successfully.
