Having problems downloading, updating, entering or managing the game? Let’s take a look at the solutions below first.

When the game server cannot be connected, new games may not be downloaded. Downloaded games can be played normally.

  1. Check the network connection.
  2. If you still cannot connect, enable VPN and try again.
  3. VPN connection failed? Please change the node, or go to Google Play to update the app.

Google Play

Please check the Android system version of your phone first. If it is lower than Android 10, please upgrade the system to Android 10 or higher and then reopen the game.

If you have upgraded to Android 10 or higher and still experience freezes, black screens, white screens, crashes or errors, please click "Game Bugs, Problem Feedback" at the bottom of the page and tell us the name of the game and the problems you encountered.

We add 2–4 new games each week. Once released, they appear in ‘All Games’ on the home screen. Check the home screen for when they become available.

Open "Settings → Downloads", find the downloaded game that you are not playing for the time being, click "More → Delete" on the right, confirm and clean up the local files of the game.

To play again, you will need to connect to the internet and download the game again. Please confirm which game you want to remove to avoid deleting it by mistake.
